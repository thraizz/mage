# goMage — Planeswalker Identity Guide

## 1. Brand Essence

### Core Idea

**goMage is the Aetherhub.** It is not just an arena; it is a spark-enabled interface for the modern Planeswalker. It bridges the gap between the tactile satisfaction of paper Magic and the sleek efficiency of the digital multiverse.

### Brand Keywords

- **WUBRG Harmony** – Respects the color wheel; balanced yet diverse.
- **The Stack & Priority** – Orderly, logical, responsive, and deep.
- **Spark-Driven** – Empowering the user (the player is the hero).
- **Oracle Clarity** – Text and UI must be as precise as card rules text.

### Overall Tone & Feel

- **Artifact Chic:** Think _Kaladesh_ invention meets _Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty_.
- **High-Fidelity Mana:** Effects should feel like drawing raw power from lands.
- **Esports Ready:** Clean enough for a Pro Tour broadcast, thematic enough for a Commander pod.

## 2. Color Palette

The palette must be strictly aligned with the **Color Wheel**, but desaturated for a modern digital UI (to avoid eye strain).

### Primary Colors (The Interface)

| **Blind Eternities Black** | `#0B0C10` | Primary background (Deep Void).                          |

### Secondary Colors (The Color Pie)

These are used for distinct game states or categorization. They must be instantly recognizable to an MTG player.

> **Note:** Always adhere to WUBRG order (White, Blue, Black, Red, Green) when listing colors or organizing filters.

## 3. Typography

### Primary Display Font (Flavor & Identity)

- **Beleren (or close approximation):** This is the distinct "Magic" font. If licensing is an issue, use **Goudy Medieval** or **Cinzel** (Bold).
- **Usage:** Headers, deck names, "Victory/Defeat" screens.

### Secondary Readability Font (The "Oracle" Text)

- **MPlantin (or crimson/serif alternative):** This is the font used on actual Magic cards. Using a serif font for body text subconsciously tells the user "this is rules text."
- **Inter / Roboto:** Use for strictly UI elements (settings, menus) where "card feel" isn't required.

### Style Guidelines

- **Keyword Bolding:** Just like on cards, key terms (**Haste**, **Trample**, **Submit**) must be bolded.
- **Italics for Flavor:** Use italics for non-functional flavor text in the UI.

## 4. Logo Direction

### Logo Components

- **The Spark:** A central burst or geometric star representing the Planeswalker spark.
- **The Pentagon:** A subtle nod to the 5 colors of magic (the pentagon on the card back).
- **The Hedron:** Use angular, stone-like geometry (inspired by Zendikar hedrons) to hold the "G".

### Style Attributes

- **Manalith Aesthetic:** Looks like a mana rock—stone texture with glowing internal energy.
- **Load State:** The logo should "fill" with mana (color) during loading screens.

---

## 5. UI/UX Visual Identity

### The "Battlefield" Moodboard

- **Dark Mode Default:** To make card art pop.
- **The Stack Visualization:** A literal vertical stack visual for notifications and modal windows.
- **Phase Indicators:** A horizontal tracker (Untap > Upkeep > Draw...) used as a progress bar or stepper.

### Interaction Feel

- **Tap Animation:** Buttons or toggles should have a slight "tap" (rotation or skew) animation when active.
- **Summoning Sickness:** Disabled elements should have a "dizzy" or swirling gray overlay.
- **Card Snap:** Elements should snap into place with a heavy, satisfying "thud" sound design and lack of bounce (simulating cardstock on a table).

---

## 6. Iconography & Illustration Style

### Iconography

- **Set Symbols:** Use shapes reminiscent of expansion symbols (rarity colors apply: Black, Silver, Gold, Orange).
- **Mechanic Icons:** Shield (Defense), Sword (Attack), Tombstone (Graveyard/History), Library (Deck/Profile).

### Illustration Style

- **"Full Art" Focus:** The UI should frame content like a "borderless" card.
- **Painterly Textures:** Avoid flat vector art. Use brush-stroke textures in backgrounds to mimic the style of classic Magic art.

## 7. Voice & Messaging

### Tone

- **Tactical & Grandiose:** You are speaking to a wizard, not a user.
- **Keywords:** Library, Hand, Graveyard, Battlefield, Exile, Spark, Mana.

### Vocabulary Shifts

- _Don't say:_ "Sign Up" $\rightarrow$ _Say:_ **"Ignite Your Spark"**
- _Don't say:_ "Loading..." $\rightarrow$ _Say:_ **"Gathering Mana..."**
- _Don't say:_ "Delete" $\rightarrow$ _Say:_ **"Exile"**
- _Don't say:_ "Search" $\rightarrow$ _Say:_ **"Tutor"** or **"Scry"**

### Examples

- **Welcome Prompt:** "Welcome to the Blind Eternities."
- **Error Message:** "Spell Fizzled. Valid target not found."
- **Success Message:** "Spell Resolved."

## 8. Practical CI Usage

### For Website

- **Hero Section:** A parallax effect moving through a "Planar Portal."
- **Feature Grid:** Laid out like a 3x3 binder page.

### For App UI

- **Avatars:** Circular frames resembling the _Arena_ or _Commander_ frame.
- **Backgrounds:** Subtle, animated textures of the five basic lands (shifting mist, falling leaves, bubbling magma).

### For Social Media

- **Spoiler Season Hype:** Announce updates using "Preview Card" graphical frames.
- **Flavor Text:** Every post should end with a short, italicized flavor quote relevant to the update.
